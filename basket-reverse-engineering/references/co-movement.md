# Co-movement evidence

Run a light check if it can support or distinguish an exposure explanation. Use actual
adjusted returns, a stated window, sample size and appropriate market/sector benchmark;
state whether adjustment means excess returns or regression residuals. Align currencies
and trading dates, and flag short histories or non-synchronous market closes where material.
Do not silently fill missing prices to create observations.

Look within hypothesised clusters and at outliers. Shared market or sector exposure is not
proof of a narrow theme; low aggregate correlation does not refute a multi-theme basket.
Keep the output to one or two lines. If skipped or unavailable, state that and the reason.
